  New workflow set (or N), then fill its Checkpoint, Text encoder, VAE, LoRA and
  Other slots from a short list that puts your own sets and your pictures'
  evidence first, or in one go with Fill from pictures and Fill from a set.

  set marked "Not on shelf", and Clone with new models offers your sets'
