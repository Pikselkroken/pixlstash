- Deleting a workflow, or deleting models from the shelf to the Trash, now
  shows a receipt with **Undo** (and Ctrl+Z). Undo takes the files back out of
  the Trash and puts the workflow or model back as it was, with its name,
  settings and versions; **Redo** deletes it again. A permanent delete
  (Shift+Delete) still has no undo. On macOS, put a model back from the Trash in
  Finder instead.
