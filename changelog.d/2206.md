- Folders inside a model folder can now be created, renamed and moved from the
  Folders tree in the Models sidebar: right-click a folder or use its ⋯ menu,
  or press F2 to rename and Ctrl Shift N for a new folder. Drag a folder onto
  another to move it. A move to another drive shows what it will copy first,
  checks every model on arrival, and runs in the corner card like a model move.
