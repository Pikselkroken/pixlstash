- In the desktop app, hold Alt (Option on a Mac) and drag pictures from the
  grid into a folder, a mail or a chat, and the whole selection arrives as the
  original files, however many there are, instead of one thumbnail. Each is
  named after when it was made, like `PixlStash-2026-10-24-14.22.42.png`, the
  same as Ctrl+C gives. A plain drag onto sets, people and stacks works as
  before.
