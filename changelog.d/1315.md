- Stacks have a Keep recipes only action beside Keep cover only. It moves the
  pictures in a stack that could be made again from their workflow to the
  Scrapheap, so once it is emptied each keeps its thumbnail and recipe. The
  confirm says why the others stay (no recorded workflow, a model not on your
  shelf, no thumbnail yet, or a ghost your privacy setting would not keep), can
  turn on keeping every ghost, and one Ctrl+Z puts it all back.
