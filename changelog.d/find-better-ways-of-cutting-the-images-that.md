- Character sheets are cut where the photos meet, whichever model drew them.
  The cut looks for the gutter or the plain background between the photos
  instead of a straight edge near where the layout put it, and keeps clear of
  the faces, so a sheet drawn off the grid or with no gutter at all is far less
  often cut through someone. The zoomed sheet outlines the six photos *Use*
  will make, and a sheet with no clear cut somewhere says *may not cut
  cleanly*.
