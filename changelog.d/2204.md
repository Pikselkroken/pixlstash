- Moving models can now target a subfolder of a model folder. Drag models
  onto a folder in the Folders tree, cut and paste them with Ctrl X and
  Ctrl V or with Cut and Paste here in the right-click menus, or pick the
  folder in Move to…. LoRAs at the root of a model folder dropped onto its
  `Lora` subfolder now move instead of being skipped.
- PixlStash's own model store no longer accepts moves in, matching the rule
  that its files are not moved out one at a time.
