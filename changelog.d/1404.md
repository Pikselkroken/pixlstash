- The Workflows view is now a grid of cards, one per workflow, with its
  pictures on the card and its models, LoRAs and type beside them. Workflows
  that are the same graph with a different character LoRA sit together in a
  stack you open in place. Sort by your ratings, by what you used last, or by
  how many pictures a workflow made.
- The Workflows list's Group, Show and Ghosts controls are gone with the list
  they belonged to. Stacks do the grouping; the rest come back as a Filters
  panel, and until then the count under the title says how many workflows are
  being left out.
- Opening a workflow from a picture's Recipe section still takes you to it,
  unless it is one of the workflows the grid leaves out — a hidden one, or one
  with fewer than three pictures that you have not rated. The grid now says so
  instead of just leaving you at the top of the list.
- Some things the old list could do are not in this view yet: setting how a
  saved workflow fills its picture inputs, deleting an imported workflow file,
  exporting a workflow's graph, and opening the pictures a workflow made.
  Workflows already set up keep running exactly as they did, and a run still
  asks you to pick a picture where it needs one.
