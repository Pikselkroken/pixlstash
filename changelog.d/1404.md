- The Workflows view is now a grid of cards, one per workflow, with its
  pictures on the card and its models, LoRAs and type beside them. Workflows
  that are the same graph with a different character LoRA sit together in a
  stack you open in place. Sort by your ratings, by what you used last, or by
  how many pictures a workflow made.
- The Workflows list's Group, Show and Ghosts controls are gone with the list
  they belonged to. Stacks do the grouping; the rest come back as a Filters
  panel, and until then the count under the title says how many workflows are
  being left out.
- Setting how a saved workflow fills its picture inputs, and deleting an
  imported workflow file, are not in this view yet. Workflows already set up
  keep running exactly as they did, and a run still asks you to pick a picture
  where it needs one.
