- Tags can be filed into groups (Character, Copyright, Meta, General, or any
  you name). Right-click a tag and choose Move to group; once a group exists,
  the picture's tags gain a By group tab that shows them under headings you
  can fold, copy and drag tags between. A group belongs to the tag, so moving
  one refiles it on every picture, and Undo takes it back.
- Tags to review gains a Groups view for filing many tags at once, and
  Filters → Tags can require or exclude a whole group ("any Character tag").
