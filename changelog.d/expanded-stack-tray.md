- Expanding a stack now opens a tray under its card instead of scattering the
  stack's pictures among the ones around them. The card stays where it was and
  becomes the tray's tab, so one unbroken panel runs from the cover down through
  the pictures it stands for, and the tray carries the stack's name, a Collapse
  and an Unstack. The faint coloured wash and the stripe along the bottom of
  each thumbnail are gone with it.
- One stack is open at a time: opening a second closes the first. "Expand all"
  has gone from the Grid view menu, which now has a single "Collapse"; the menu
  says "1 open" while a tray is out. Esc closes it too.
