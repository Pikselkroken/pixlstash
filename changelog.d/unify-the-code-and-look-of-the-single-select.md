- The lightbox's Info tab is now the same panel the grid's side panel shows
  for one picture: its faces under a small thumbnail, the tag field T puts the
  caret in, tags shaded by how sure the tagger was, Rejected, the description
  and Properties. Properties lists the picture's ID, file, size, aspect, type,
  frames, runtime, model, quality, date and the sets that lock it, in both.
- Tags can be picked in the grid's side panel too, not only in the lightbox:
  click a tag (Ctrl- or Shift-click one that only some of the pictures have)
  to copy the picked ones with Ctrl+C or remove them with Delete. Dragging a
  tag onto Rejected rejects it, and dragging a rejected one onto the tags
  accepts it, in both.
- Rejected tags the tagger was less than 25% sure of fold behind a link, which
  shows them all. Your hidden tags stay hidden in the side panel as they do in
  the lightbox.
