- A training run can be trained longer from any step it kept: finished,
  stopped or failed. **Train longer** on the run's page, in its card's
  right-click menu or on the selection pill picks the step and how many more
  steps to train. The same run carries on with the same pictures and
  settings, and its new checkpoints join its stack on the shelf. From the
  final step the optimizer carries on too. Workflows that load the run's final
  file get the longer-trained one.
