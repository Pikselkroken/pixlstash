- Pictures in the grid now open with a double-click, and a single click
  selects. Prefer the old way? Settings › Appearance › Mouse › Single click.
- The picture viewer is quicker to leave: click the dark area around the
  picture, or middle-click anywhere. Esc still works.
- Choose whether the mouse wheel zooms the viewer or steps through pictures
  (Settings › Appearance › Mouse). Ctrl+wheel always zooms and Shift+wheel
  always steps, whichever you pick.
