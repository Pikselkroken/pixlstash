- Workflows has its own sidebar: All workflows, Favourites, Recently used,
  ComfyUI templates (with how many are new), and Type and Base model filters
  with counts. Add workflow is the button at its foot.
- The Workflows toolbar is for browsing only: sort, Filter with the active
  filters as removable chips, and a Search field (F). The selection bar offers
  Show pictures, Open in ComfyUI and Export, with everything else under ⋯.
- The workflow panel on the right is rearranged into collapsible Models,
  LoRAs, Parameters, GPU memory and Latest pictures sections. LoRAs and
  parameters are edited in their own dialogs, opened from each section's
  pencil. Run… is the button at its foot, and R runs the selected workflow.
- Recipes show each recipe's picture and what it changes, seed first.
- The workflow panel no longer has a Tasks tab; running work is under Tasks
  in the title bar. Favourites moved from the Filter menu to the sidebar.
