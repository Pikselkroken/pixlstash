- Workflows that differed only by leftover nodes, a text box, film grain, a
  loader variant (GGUF, multi-GPU, the PixlStash shelf loaders) or seed
  variance are now one workflow. Their names, notes, defaults and saved recipes
  move with them.
- Seed variance is a stage like upscale: on or off in a workflow's default
  recipe, and switchable per run.
