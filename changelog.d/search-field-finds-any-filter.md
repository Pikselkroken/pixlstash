- The toolbar search is now the field itself: click it or press F and it
  widens in place. Typing lists every filter whose name matches (tags, faces,
  checkpoints, LoRAs, workflows, favourites, problems), not only tags. Enter
  searches the words; Tab adds the highlighted filter and Shift+Tab its
  opposite (Lacks, for a tag), replacing Enter, Shift+Enter and Ctrl+Enter.
  Every active filter shows as a token in the field, and the Filter button
  counts them.
