- **Convert to Inpainting workflow**, in a workflow card's menu, makes a new
  workflow from an image-to-image or image-edit one that redraws only the area
  you select, at the workflow's own working size, and blends it back into the
  untouched picture. The workflow you converted is left as it was. It needs
  the ComfyUI-Inpaint-CropAndStitch node pack.
