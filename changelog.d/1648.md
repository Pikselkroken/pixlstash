- Close-up face defects (malformed eyes and teeth, flux chin) are detected more
  reliably. The quality check now looks at the whole face at a higher
  resolution instead of a fixed-size patch that cut into most faces.
- New "Quality crop" option in the PixlStash tagger's settings (the gear beside
  the tagger in Settings > Behaviour): 512p (default, most accurate), 320p
  (faster) or Off. It applies to pictures tagged from then on; pictures already
  tagged keep their tags until they are re-tagged.
