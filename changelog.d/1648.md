- New "Whole-face close-up" option in the PixlStash tagger's settings. When on,
  the tagger checks faces for malformed eyes, malformed teeth and flux chin on
  a close-up of the whole face at a higher resolution, instead of a small fixed
  window that cuts off the chin or an eye on larger faces. It is more accurate
  but makes tagging slower, so it is off by default.
  Turning it on offers to re-check the pictures you already have with faces;
  only their face quality tags are redone, and other tags are kept.
