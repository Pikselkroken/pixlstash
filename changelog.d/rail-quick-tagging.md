- Tagging moved from the Tag dialog into the side panel. Select pictures and
  press T: the caret lands in the panel's tag field, and each tag you type and
  Enter goes on every selected picture, ready for the next. A tag's shading
  shows how many of the selected pictures have it, or for one picture how sure
  the tagger was; click a partly shaded tag to add it to the rest. Watermarks
  and other flaws show in red at the front.
- With one picture selected the side panel shows its faces with their names
  under a small thumbnail, and its description gets the rest of the height.
