- The Tags view has a Reviews panel on the right with three tabs: your open
  reviews with their scope, the archived receipts, and your stickers. The
  archived list and the sticker shelf no longer sit under the tag board.
- A review now has one way to end: End review…. It asks whether to keep or
  undo the changes, and keeping them files a receipt under Archived. Abort
  review… and Archive review are gone. A review with no decisions ends at once.
