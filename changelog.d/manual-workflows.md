- Workflows you import, pull from ComfyUI or make yourself are now manual
  workflows: each is its own entry on the Workflows grid, badged Manual, and
  is never folded into a workflow PixlStash grouped from your pictures. A
  duplicate, a fixed copy, a clone or a LoRA edit says which workflow it came
  from. Pictures you make with a manual workflow count on it, and deleting it
  keeps them: they go back to the workflow their graph belongs to. Workflow
  files you imported before are carried over as manual workflows.
- A saved recipe can be turned into a workflow of its own with Extract
  workflow, even when the workflow it was saved on is gone.
