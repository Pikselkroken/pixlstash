- **Generate a character's training pictures.** *Create a training set…* can now draw the pictures: describe the character, draw 2–4 character sheets (four passport photos and two body photos of one person each) with a MOSS-Image workflow, optionally with the character's LoRA, at one of three sizes and four body framings (waist-up to full body), zoom in on them, and keep the sheets that look right. Their photos become the character's pictures, marked *new* in the picker, with a *New only* filter. The sheets themselves never join the library. A character with no pictures opens straight on this step.
