- Smart sets: with filters on, *Save as smart set…* makes a set that fills
  itself with every picture those filters match, and keeps up as you import,
  tag and score. It sits in the sidebar with your other sets and works
  wherever they do: share links, export, training and runs. Pictures you add
  or take out by hand stay that way until *Reset to automatic*, and locking a
  smart set freezes the pictures it has.
