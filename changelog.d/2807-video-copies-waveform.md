- A video your browser can't play, such as ProRes, MOV or MKV, now gets a
  playable copy made once with the bundled ffmpeg and kept with your library.
  Short clips are made straight away; a long one is offered first, with how
  long it will take.
- A clip with sound shows its waveform in a thin lane under its frames, with
  the playhead running through both. Click it, or use the left and right
  arrow keys, to jump to a point in the clip.
- Settings > Libraries & imports has a Video copies section when the open
  library has copies. It shows how many there are and how much space they
  take, and Delete copies clears them. A deleted copy is made again when its
  clip is next opened.
