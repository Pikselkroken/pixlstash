- Training learns your machine. The first run of a setup checks memory and
  speed with a few short steps before it trains, stepping to more offloading if
  it runs out of GPU memory, and every run after it is fitted to what was
  measured. The Train dialog then gives the time ("About 50 min") and says when
  a figure was measured here. When previews would not fit beside training, they
  are drawn from each checkpoint once training is done.
