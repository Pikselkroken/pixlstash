- The Filters menu is rebuilt as a menu of filter kinds, each opening its own
  menu beside it, and it stays open while you choose. Every choice shows how
  many pictures in the current view it matches on its own.
- Active filters now show as chips in a strip under the toolbar, each with its
  own ×, and Clear all at the end.
- A score range can include unscored pictures: "Score 3–4 or unscored".
