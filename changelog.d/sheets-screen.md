- **Character sheets have a screen of their own, and nothing is lost by
  leaving it.** Sheets are drawn under the character, People › name › Sheets,
  instead of inside *Create a training set…*. Every draw is kept on the
  character: close the screen or reload and the sheets, the ones still
  drawing and your kept marks are all there when you come back. A **Sheets**
  row under the character in the sidebar shows how the newest draw is going,
  every *Character sheet* run in Tasks has an *Open sheets* link, and the
  character's menu has *Draw sheets…*. Earlier draws stay listed below the
  newest, so a sheet can still be kept later.
