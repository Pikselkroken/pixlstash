- Runs can add a tested photo look after your prompt: camera, light, and skin
  and makeup, each a preset or your own words. It is on by default for
  MiniMax H3 Image, so a short prompt no longer comes out as a glossy render,
  and off by default everywhere else. Your own words stay first and
  untouched, and a picture's recipe shows them apart from what was added.
  Agents can ask for it with `prompt_assist` on `run_workflow`.
