- Fixed: if your library's folder is deleted, moved, renamed or on a drive that
  is not connected, the desktop app no longer stops at an error. It shows what
  is missing and lets you find the folder, open another library, start a new
  one, or try again. No pictures are deleted by any of these.
- `pixlstash-cli libraries activate` chooses the library PixlStash opens, for
  when the active one is missing and Settings cannot be reached. `relocate
  --same-library` refuses a folder holding a different library.
