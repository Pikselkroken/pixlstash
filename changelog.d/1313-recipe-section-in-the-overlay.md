- The picture viewer's sidebar has a Recipe section for anything made in
  ComfyUI: the models it used, with the strength each LoRA was loaded at and a
  tick beside any file the recipe named exactly; the prompt; the sampler
  settings; and the pictures the run actually loaded as inputs. Click a model to
  open it on the model shelf, or open the workflow in Workflows. Generate
  variants is there too, so you can re-run a picture without leaving the viewer.
- The workflow JSON, with its Copy and Download buttons, has moved out of
  Metadata and into the new Recipe section, beside everything else about how the
  picture was made.
