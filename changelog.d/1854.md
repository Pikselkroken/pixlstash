- PixlStash no longer reads ComfyUI's saved workflows by itself. Pre-release
  builds of 1.12 pulled every workflow ComfyUI had saved, once a minute; that
  is gone, with its "Pull workflows from ComfyUI" switch in Settings ›
  ComfyUI. A workflow now comes in from ComfyUI only when you send it: import
  it, or drop its file in the watched workflows folder. Workflows a
  pre-release build already pulled stay as ordinary workflows, and Filters ›
  Origin › ComfyUI on the Workflows screen lists them together if you want to
  clear them out.
