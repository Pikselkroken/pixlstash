- Videos the browser can't play (ProRes, many MOV, MKV and AVI files) now play
  in the lightbox. PixlStash makes an MP4 copy once and keeps it; a short clip's
  copy starts by itself, and a long one is offered with an estimate of how long
  it takes. Settings › Libraries & imports shows the copies and can delete them.
- Clips with sound show a waveform under their frames in the lightbox. Click it
  or use the arrow keys to seek.
- Settings › Libraries & imports: "Add a library…" sits on the Libraries
  heading, and the library and import-folder lists scroll in their own boxes.
