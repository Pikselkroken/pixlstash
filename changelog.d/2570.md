- Fixed: cropping a picture down to a face no longer costs it as much of its
  Quality score. A picture with a face is now judged on how many pixels the
  face gets rather than on the size of the whole file, so a crop and the
  picture it came from get about the same credit for resolution, and a
  full-length shot with a small face scores lower. Scores of pictures with a
  face are recalculated in the background after the update.
