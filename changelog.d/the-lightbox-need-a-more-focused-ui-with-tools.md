- The lightbox is calmer: its toolbar now only gets you around (back, previous
  and next, Set, Project, segmentation boxes, compare, zoom, full screen). Every tool moved into
  the inspector tab it belongs to: rotate, crop, upscale and filters are Quick
  changes in Edit, the paint tools sit at the top of Edit, and the rating
  heads Info. Close the inspector and only the picture is left.
- Press F in the lightbox for a real full screen. Point at the top edge for
  the controls, press I for the inspector, and Esc to leave.
- Face boxes no longer need switching on. Point at a face in Info, or at a
  tag the object detector found, to see its box; hold Shift to see them all.
  Segment turns on the toolbar's Segmentation boxes, so its boxes show when
  it finishes; press it again to hide them.
- In the Edit tab, − and + change the brush size while the brush or eraser is
  in hand.
