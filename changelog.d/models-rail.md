- The Models screen's right rail is now Models | Tasks. The Models tab lists
  every model on your shelf that a workflow set can hold (checkpoints, LoRAs,
  VAEs, text encoders and unclassified files, not PixlStash's own engines)
  and says which of your workflow sets each one is already in. Drag models from it onto a set you made, onto one slot of an
  open set, or onto New workflow set, or select them and press Enter or Add.
  *Fits <set>* and *In no set yet* narrow the list, and the open set's
  *Models · N fit* button opens the rail with Fits on. A run of adds gets one
  receipt, and Undo takes all of them back.
- The right-rail button in every toolbar has a new icon, shows when the rail
  is open, and is named for what the rail holds on that screen.
