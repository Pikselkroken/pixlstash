- A workflow is now the graph, and the checkpoint, LoRAs and settings you run
  it with are its recipe. Each workflow has a default recipe, read off your
  best-rated pictures, and a run starts from it: the Run popup lets you pick
  another checkpoint and switch an upscale or FaceDetailer pass off for that
  run. Your workflows' names, notes, settings and saved recipes are carried
  over the first time this version starts; a saved recipe keeps the
  checkpoint it was saved with.
- **Merge** on the Workflows selection bar puts two or more workflows
  together, and a merged workflow's ⋯ menu splits one of its graphs back out.
  To see a workflow's pictures made with one checkpoint or LoRA, open its
  pictures and add the model or LoRA filter.
