- A new Guide in the title bar (or F1) shows what PixlStash can do: every
  feature as an illustrated card you can search or browse by goal or
  workspace, each opening to its tasks. Show me walks you through a task on
  the real screen by ringing the control to click. Cards your library cannot
  try yet are greyed and say what comes first, but still open. Keyboard
  shortcuts moved to `?`.
- A short welcome shows a few things to try on the first launch with
  pictures, and again after an update that adds something new. It replaces
  the 2.0 What's changed checklist and training tour.
