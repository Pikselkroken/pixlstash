What it is

A smarter home for your images

PixlStash is a self-hosted image management platform that automatically imports, tags and indexes your entire library.

Search, tag, drop the rubbish, and keep your best work. Access across your devices. Right from your browser or desktop app!

Built for

Anyone drowning in images

Whether you're a photographer culling shoots, an AI trainer curating datasets or a hobbyist with a large image collection, PixlStash helps you bring order to the chaos.

Handle tens of thousands of images with ease, and spend more time creating and less time organizing.

Coming from Immich?

Immich is a great self-hosted home for your photos. PixlStash does a different job.

PixlStash is a tool for working with images, not just storing them: sorting photos, maintaining tags, prepping a dataset, and running images through generation tools. Yes, it can also work as a home server.

With PixlStash you get:

  • A tag review queue, so auto-tags are verified before they're trusted.
  • Segmentation, selectable taggers, and a choice of GPU or CPU backend.
  • Two-way ComfyUI integration: control ComfyUI from PixlStash, or call PixlStash's nodes from your own workflows. Generated images return tagged, with People, Picture Set, and Project associations attached.
  • A model overview. Keep track of your diffusion models.
  • And a whole host of other work features
