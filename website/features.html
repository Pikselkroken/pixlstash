AI features

Semantic search, face recognition, tagging, captioning, scoring and a review queue that checks the models' work. All of it runs on your machine.

Semantic search

Describe what you are looking for and get pictures back, tagged or not. CLIP embeddings, computed locally. Text in documents, receipts and screenshots is read too, and every search matches it.

Face recognition

Faces are detected, embedded and grouped into people you name. Sort the grid by likeness to a person to find the pictures they are in, or search the library by one face.

Tagging and captions

PixlStash Tagger, WD14, Florence-2 or JoyCaption, chosen per request rather than per install. Review and edit the tags and description, or re-tag any selection from the context menu.

Label review queue

A ranked queue of the tags the models most likely got wrong or missed, one question per picture, answered with a click or a key. A health board per tag shows where to look next.

Object detection

Detect the objects in a picture and draw labelled boxes over them. Export the boxes as COCO or Ideogram JSON for training and annotation pipelines.

Context menus and reverse search

Right-click a picture to tag, assign, share or delete it without leaving the view, or to find visually similar pictures and other pictures of the same face.

Workflows and recipes

The ComfyUI workflows behind your pictures, as a library you can run, the recipe behind every picture you made, and the models a workflow needs, downloaded where you want them.

A Workflows screen

Every workflow your pictures were made with, plus the files you add, as a card covered with the pictures it made. The one-offs are folded away until you ask for them. Drop a workflow .json on the screen to add it.

A recipe on every picture

The workflow, prompt, models, LoRA strengths, settings and seed behind a picture made in ComfyUI, A1111 or Forge. Videos too. Save the look as a recipe and run it again, or open a workflow's pictures in one click.

One Run window

Run a workflow, make more like a picture, or run a workflow on a selection from the same window, with every value filled in and editable.

Create with LoRA

One click in Settings installs the PixlStash nodes into your ComfyUI and links the two apps. Then right-click a person or set to make new pictures with their LoRA, filed back under them.

Two people in one picture

Pick two people in the Run window, describe them both, and get one picture with both in it.

Open in ComfyUI, save back

Open a workflow in ComfyUI with its models loading off your shelf, and Ctrl+S saves it back to PixlStash as a new version. The version menu takes you back to any earlier one.

Pin or expose parameters

Lock a parameter and the Run window stops asking for it, or expose any setting of any node under a name you choose.

Workflows that fix themselves

Swap a missing checkpoint, VAE or text encoder for one on your shelf. A missing LoRA or custom seed node no longer stops a run.

Workflow sets

A card per checkpoint lists every VAE, text encoder and LoRA that has run beside it, and picks up new ones by itself. Use a set to swap a workflow's models in one go.

Every ComfyUI template

A Templates tab lists the templates your ComfyUI serves, with the files each needs and the ones you have. One you add loads from your library and shelf.

Missing models, downloaded

Download a missing model or LoRA from inside PixlStash, into the Download folder you set in Settings › Model folders, straight onto your shelf.

Runs wait for the GPU

A run that does not fit in GPU memory waits for room instead of running out of it, and PixlStash steps aside for ComfyUI.

Runs in the Tasks tab

A run keeps its row in the Tasks tab until ComfyUI is done, and the results come back tagged, with their people, sets and projects already attached. A failed run says which node failed.

Inpaint, crop and compare

Mark part of a picture in the Edit tab, say what goes there, and an inpaint workflow redraws only that part. Three come with PixlStash, Qwen Image 2.1, Qwen Image Edit 2511 and FLUX.2 Klein 9B, and your own inpaint workflows show up in the same list. Cropping needs no ComfyUI at all.

Paint a selection

Rectangle, ellipse, lasso, brush, eraser and colour wand, then grow, feather or fill the holes. A before/after slider shows what changed.

One edit after another

An axe head for one gnome, blue lipstick for another. Each edit is a new picture in the original's stack, so the next one can start while the last is still running.

Compare any two pictures

Select two pictures in the grid and choose Compare, or right-click a second one in the viewer's filmstrip. One is laid over the other with a slider, whether or not one was made from the other.

The folders you already have

Point PixlStash at a folder and it reads it where it sits, turning the folder names you already chose into projects, people and sets, without moving, renaming or copying a file.

Add a library from Settings

One folder picker, and the folder answers. A library you already made is added as it is, a folder of pictures is counted and offered, and an empty folder starts a fresh library. A folder another library already covers is refused, by name.

Read what your folders mean

PixlStash proposes what each folder level is: project, set, person, tag or just a folder, from what the pictures in it show. You make the final decision.

Nothing written until you agree

The review screen names every project, set and person your folders are about to become, and says plainly that no file is copied, moved or renamed. An interrupted import resumes.

A new library's first screen

An empty library offers three ways in: a folder you already have, pictures dropped onto the window, or a ComfyUI to connect.

Export straight into a folder

Export the pictures you selected into a folder on the machine running PixlStash. Captions, resolution, sidecars and face crops come out as the zip export produces them, without the round trip through downloads.

Libraries, models and the command line

Keep separate picture libraries in one installation, catalogue the models already on your disks, and run the housekeeping from a terminal.

Several picture libraries

Register more than one library and keep one open at a time. Pictures, tags, scores, snapshots, API tokens and share links stay with their library; your account, preferences and model shelf stay with the installation. Move pictures from one library to another.

Model shelf

Register the folders holding your checkpoints and LoRAs and see what is in them: kind, base model, size, and every folder a copy sits in. Group and sort the shelf, move a model between folders, or delete one to your Trash.

Training runs and versions

Read an ai-toolkit run where it was written, then import it with its sample images. Versions of one subject group behind a single row.

A command line

pixlstash-cli manages libraries, writes and restores backups, and installs plugins. Every command documents itself, including the exit codes a script needs, so a nightly backup is one cron line.

Plugins

Extend PixlStash with Python plugins for captioning and image filters. Find a published one by name and install it from the command line, then apply filters to a selection from the grid.

Folder permission repair

The desktop app checks at start-up whether another account on the computer can write to your library folder, and offers to tighten the permissions. Your own access is left alone.

Work with confidence

Reverse everyday edits, review duplicate candidates, roll back to a snapshot, and keep people and sets organised across projects.

Undo, redo, and history

Reverse soft-deletion, tagging, description edits, assignments, moves and duplicate decisions with the usual shortcuts or the toolbar. Open the history to step back several changes at once.

Duplicate review

Work through likely duplicate groups in a dedicated queue: compare, choose a cover, stack the group or keep it separate, and undo the decision if it was not right. A similarity threshold sets how broadly PixlStash searches.

Stack cleanup

Filter a view to stacked or unstacked pictures. Keep cover only previews a cleanup, keeps the stack's cover, and sends the other copies to the recoverable Scrapheap.

Stacks that no longer fit

Stacks whose pictures no longer look like one another are flagged in a review queue of their own.

Multi-project organisation

Add one person or picture set to several projects. Its pictures appear in each, and removing one membership leaves the others alone.

Suggest more pictures

Start from a person or a picture set and rank the library by likeness. Adjust the match, review the result, and assign what was found in one action.

Lock a set when it is done

Lock a finished picture set to freeze its pictures' tags, descriptions and scores, for eval sets and training data that must not drift.

Snapshots & restore

Automatic, compressed snapshots of your metadata, on a daily, weekly and monthly schedule. Roll back to any restore point; a safety snapshot is taken first. Deleted pictures wait in the Scrapheap, which can empty itself after 30 to 120 days; that is off by default.

Selective restore

Restore just the pictures you select from a snapshot. Anything not selected is left untouched.

Privacy controls

Update checks and anonymous install telemetry have their own controls. Both are off until you enable them, and the Privacy pane states what is and is not sent.

Browse and organise

Image grid

A fast picture grid with scoring, stacking and sorting.

Justified or square layout

Thumbnails can keep their own aspect ratio and fill each row, or stay as squares. One size control in the toolbar, from Tiny to Huge, sets the thumbnail size in both layouts.

Batch operations

Select pictures, then tag them, assign them to sets or people, or run any batch action on them at once.

Tag autocompletion

Tag with a keyboard shortcut and let PixlStash suggest tags as you type.

People

Name the people in your pictures and keep their pictures together.

Lots of keyboard shortcuts

Lots of convenient keyboard shortcuts for navigation, selection, scoring, tagging, and more.

Statistics sidebar

Tag distribution, co-occurrences, tag confidence and picture statistics for whatever the grid is showing.

Boolean set operations

Ctrl-click multiple characters or picture sets, then combine them with Union, Overlap, Difference, or Unique (XOR) operations.

Other features

The rest of the toolkit. The AI features have their own page.

Smart score

Sort a large set by what is worth looking at, with a calibrated penalty for malformed anatomy and render defects.

Picture sharing

Share pictures, sets, people and projects with a read-only link, no account needed to view. Links are scoped and revocable, and can carry your own watermark.

Custom icons for sets

Give each picture set its own icon and colour so it stands out in the sidebar.

A folder layout for what comes next

Give a library a layout, with folders for projects, people or sets, and new pictures are written where it says. A picture moves only when its folder stops being true; Move to match is offered, counted first, and undone as one batch.

Favourites

Press H on a model, workflow set or workflow and it comes first in every list you pick from.

AI agents

pixlstash-mcp lets an AI agent search your library and read recipes, with a token from Connect AI agent.

Background imports

Imports complete on the server, so closing the tab does not cancel them. Progress shows in the Tasks tab, and a running import can be aborted from there.

Security notifications

PixlStash checks for known security issues and tells you in the title bar when a fix is available, with its severity.

Your GPU, used well

Models stay resident for a pass, decoding happens off the GPU worker, and the VRAM budget follows your card. Pick which GPU to use on a machine with more than one; Apple Silicon Macs tag, search and match faces on the GPU too.
