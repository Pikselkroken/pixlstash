FFmpeg, bundled with PixlStash
==============================

PixlStash ships an unmodified build of FFmpeg and runs it as a separate
program (command line and pipes), to make playable copies of videos and to
draw audio waveforms. It is never linked into PixlStash.

Licence
  The FFmpeg in this build is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public
  License, version 3 or later (macOS build: 2.1 or later; configured without
  --enable-gpl and --enable-nonfree). The licence texts are in
  COPYING.LGPLv3 and COPYING.GPLv3 next to this file.

  H.264 is encoded with Cisco's OpenH264 (libopenh264, BSD-2-Clause) on
  Windows and Linux, and with Apple's VideoToolbox on macOS. No GPL encoder
  (libx264, libx265) is included.

Corresponding source
  The FFmpeg source, the OpenH264 source and the build scripts used for the
  exact binary shipped here are attached to the GitHub release named in the
  build pin (packaging/ffmpeg/ffmpeg_pin.json, field "release") of
  https://github.com/Pikselkroken/pixlstash/releases, as ffmpeg-source-*.tar.gz
  together with SOURCES.txt, which names each commit.
  The upstream projects are https://ffmpeg.org/, https://github.com/cisco/openh264
  and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds.

  You may replace this binary: set PIXLSTASH_FFMPEG to the path of another
  ffmpeg and PixlStash uses that one instead.
